# 第一卷人物素材盘点与立绘

## 素材盘点

- 31 位可招募伙伴各有独立立绘，包括 27 位原著人物与雷铮、阮青篙、沈青黛、松岩子四位家世伙伴。
- 主线、历练、重逢和丐帮考核中，另有 20 位已配置出场的原著人物拥有独立立绘。
- 忘机客等 15 位 NPC 拥有独立立绘，草寇、兵卒等八类常见敌人各有一张共用立绘。
- 男女主角两张全身立绘已按同一画风重绘。见 [主界面美术素材](./ui-art-prompts.md)。

74 张伙伴与 NPC 立绘由 Codex 内置 ImageGen 绘制，存放在 [人物素材目录](../frontend/src/assets/portraits/)。男女主角立绘位于 [界面素材目录](../frontend/src/assets/)。规格均为 800 × 1200 透明 WebP。武库、客栈、结缘与战斗角色卡按角色 ID 或姓名匹配。基础和觉醒形态共用立绘。

按小说描写取材，脸型、姿态及书中没有交代的服饰细节由美术设计补足。目录参照 [远流《射鵰英雄傳》](https://www.ylib.com/activity/YLib2026/book-info.asp?itemNo=D7B0C)，赵志敬的形象取自《神雕侠侣》。

## 绘制提示词

每张图独立生成。组合统一描述和对应的角色提示词。陆乘风使用坐姿构图，其余人物使用站姿构图。

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: finished transparent character standing portrait for a Chinese wuxia RPG set in the Southern Song period, placed over a painted landscape inside a horizontal game UI. Premium original painterly realistic Chinese martial-arts illustration, natural anatomy and faces, finely observed fabric, subtly visible hand-painted brush grain, local color in a reserved historical earth-and-ink palette, soft diffuse upper-left daylight and modest warm rim light. Tall 2:3 portrait, centered full figure from head to the soles of both boots, comfortable 6% margin around every prop and the complete silhouette, camera around chest height; standing, three-quarter angle toward viewer's right, readable face and silhouette at small sizes. Render the actual alpha-transparent background on all pixels around the figure, no ground, scenery, shadow plane, glow, typography, borders, watermark or other people. Give the character an original face and wardrobe interpretation, independent of any screen adaptation, actor, game artwork or existing illustration.
```
